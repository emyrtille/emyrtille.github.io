## 01 / PERFORMANCE & MODERNIZATION

### Make existing systems *better.*

I've contributed to modernizing high-traffic backend services, including migrations from PHP to Go.

On one project, I worked directly on API design and development, helping translate complex existing behavior into a new implementation. I also contributed to client migrations, A/B testing and production monitoring.

On another, I coordinated a technical migration with an external engineering team. I helped clarify existing rules, plan the transition and organize testing, monitoring and progressive rollouts with the teams involved.

Both projects delivered substantial improvements in performance and infrastructure efficiency. Those outcomes were the result of collective engineering efforts, with different contributions from me on each project.

## 02 / OBSERVABILITY & DEBUGGING

### Make problems *visible.*

I built monitoring dashboards and alerting with New Relic and Terraform to make application behavior easier to investigate.

That work helped uncover a long-standing production issue, which the team was then able to resolve. Building the observability was my direct contribution; diagnosing and fixing the underlying issue was a team effort.

I like creating visibility that developers can actually use, whether they are investigating an incident or checking how a change behaves after deployment.

## 03 / INVESTIGATING & MEASURING SYSTEMS

### Understand before *changing.*

When exploring improvements to an existing system, I start by understanding its current behavior and constraints.

I have audited codebases, explored architectural alternatives and proposed ways to measure quality before committing to larger changes. In a recent initiative, I used technical exploration and prototyping to help frame possible next steps.

That broader redesign remains exploratory, and the measurement work is still in progress. I see value in that distinction: not every investigation needs to become a rewrite, but it should help the team make a better-informed decision.

## MY APPROACH

### Observe, understand, *improve.*

I prefer evidence over assumptions. Sometimes that means implementing better monitoring, sometimes contributing code to a more efficient service, and sometimes gathering enough information to decide whether a change is worth making.

For me, reliability is part of everyday software engineering, from development through production.
