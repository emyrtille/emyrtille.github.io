## 01 / CROSS-TEAM DELIVERY

### Keep complex changes *moving.*

I helped coordinate the migration of an established backend service, working with an external engineering team and internal stakeholders.

My role was to make existing behavior understandable, clarify requirements and help organize the transition. I worked with the teams involved on testing, monitoring and progressive rollouts, connecting technical decisions with a workable delivery plan.

That experience reinforced how much successful delivery depends on shared understanding, clear responsibilities and coordination across teams.

## 02 / PRODUCT & ENGINEERING DELIVERY

### Shape the plan, *deliver the work.*

As a Squad Engineer, I work closely with the Product Manager to shape our roadmap and share responsibility for the team's delivery.

I contribute to defining priorities, breaking down work, tracking progress and making trade-offs as requirements evolve. I also help identify technical risks, surface dependencies and make their implications visible when decisions need to be made.

I stay involved in development alongside the team, connecting planning and technical decisions with the reality of building and delivering software.

## 03 / TECHNICAL COMMUNITIES

### Learn together, *share what matters.*

I helped facilitate an internal API engineering community, sharing technical updates and encouraging discussions across teams.

Outside work, I'm involved with AFUP, the French PHP community, where I help organize conferences and local meetups.

I value these opportunities to exchange ideas, learn from engineers with different experiences and contribute to a wider technical community.

## MY APPROACH

### Stay close to *the work.*

I enjoy contributing to the bigger picture while remaining a hands-on engineer.

Whether I'm writing code, coordinating a technical transition or working through delivery decisions with teammates, I try to keep things practical, understandable and focused on building useful software.
